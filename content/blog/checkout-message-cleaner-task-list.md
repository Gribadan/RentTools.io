Your checkout template may have started as a short reminder, then grown whenever a turnover problem appeared. A linen request here, a supply check there, and the guest eventually receives something closer to the cleaner’s work list.

Before sending it again, separate what the guest needs to know to leave from what your team needs to prepare the home. The two messages serve different people and need different checks.

## Check which policy and booking you are reviewing

Airbnb’s [reasonable house rules policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/4168) applies to reservations booked on or after November 20, 2026. It generally disallows tasks normally performed by a cleaner; review its context-specific exceptions.

That is a booking-date condition, not simply the date the guest checks out. For an existing reservation, review the applicable terms and the information provided when it was booked. Do not assume that editing a saved template settles which rules apply to that guest.

Keep this review narrow. You are preparing a departure reminder, not trying to resolve a dispute, impose a new charge or rewrite the booking through a message.

## Write the reminder for the person leaving

Start with the verified checkout arrangement for this stay. Check the correct home, date and time, then make the practical key-return or departure route clear. Remove staff-only details that do not help the guest complete that handover.

Airbnb’s [checkout guide](https://www.airbnb.com/resources/hosting-homes/a/a-clear-and-simple-checkout-584) describes listing rules and requests that guests can see before booking. Use a reminder to restate relevant information, rather than append an internal work list.

**Illustrative wording, to adapt after checking the reservation:** “Thank you for staying. Your checkout time is the one shown in your reservation. Please use the key-return arrangement already provided. If any detail is unclear, message us before you leave.”

This is a starting point, not a ready-made instruction for every home. Replace vague references with the correct existing arrangement when useful. Do not add an uncertain time, a guessed key location or an extra task just to fill out the template.

## Keep the turnover work with the team

Prepare a separate instruction for the person doing the cleaning. It can describe the rooms used, linen needed, supplies to check and any open issue for the next arrival. Assign the work and the next confirmation instead of passing the internal checklist to the departing guest.

**Illustrative situation:** the next stay needs a different bed setup. The host verifies that setup and briefs the cleaner. Adding it to the departing guest’s reminder would confuse who is responsible for preparing the next stay.

If the team already has a copied schedule, use the [schedule-copy review](/blog/cleaning-schedule-copy-refresh) to replace outdated instructions clearly. A short guest message and a detailed team handover can coexist without being combined into one long list.

## Review the saved template and the final message

RentTools stores property message templates. Use a saved template as a draft to review for the particular reservation and language. Check the final wording, not just the template name: an old body can survive behind a current-looking title.

Saving a template does not update Airbnb’s listing rules or send the reminder for you. Review the destination platform and send the checked message through your chosen guest communication channel. If another reminder is already scheduled there, make sure the guest will not receive contradictory instructions.

Keep the cleaner’s brief separate in the appropriate work record. RentTools cleaning records support a date, status and notes; use the correct property and cleaning date, with unresolved work and the person who will confirm it.

## Confirm the handover after the message

A sent reminder is not evidence that the guest has left, returned the key or completed the agreed handover. If departure is uncertain, use the [checkout-confirmation check](/blog/checkout-confirmation-cleaning-handover) before relying on a calendar date to send someone inside.

If the key is missing, handle the [key-return question](/blog/missing-checkout-key-handover) as its own follow-up. Do not mark cleaning complete because a departure reminder was sent or answered.
