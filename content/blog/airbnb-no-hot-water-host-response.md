A guest says the shower is cold. The cleaner has just left, another arrival is due tomorrow, and the technician has not answered. The useful response is a coordinated plan: who checks the fault, when the guest hears back, and whether the next stay can still go ahead.

This guide is an operational workflow for hosts, not a repair manual or a fixed refund tariff. The example messages below are templates, not accounts of a real stay.

## Confirm the scope before promising a solution

Ask whether the problem affects the shower, other taps, or the whole property, and when it started. Establish whether someone can attend and whether the guest is comfortable arranging access. A photo of a displayed fault code can help a qualified technician; asking a guest to dismantle or repair equipment cannot substitute for a maintenance visit.

Appoint one coordinator, even if the cleaner, manager and technician are all involved. Otherwise the guest can receive three different arrival times while nobody checks whether anyone actually attended.

Keep a short incident record: the report time, affected facilities, person responsible, next promised update and next booking. Record facts separately from estimates. “Technician contacted” and “repair completed” are different states.

## Give the guest an update they can use

Reply with what you know, what you are doing and when you will return with an update. Choose a time you can meet even if there is no repair progress.

An adaptable message:

> Thank you for reporting the hot-water problem. I have contacted maintenance and am confirming access and timing. I will update you by [time], even if the repair time is still unconfirmed. Please tell me if this affects your plans so we can discuss an alternative.

Keep the important updates in the reservation message thread, including a summary after any phone call. Airbnb's [issue-reporting guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/248) asks guests to document problems and contact their host. A clear written timeline helps both sides explain what happened.

Do not describe the problem as minor before understanding its effect on this guest. A working kitchen tap does not resolve an unusable shower.

## Make the repair decision before the next arrival

Use the actual repair outlook and the guest's needs to choose a next step:

| Situation | Next host action |
| --- | --- |
| Attendance confirmed; completion still unknown | Explain the uncertainty and agree the next update. |
| Repair cannot be verified before the next arrival | Contact that guest and the booking platform; discuss suitable arrangements before arrival. |
| Repair reported complete | Obtain a functional check and complete the property handover before reopening. |

Do not let an unanswered message become an assumption that a guest accepts the disruption. If continuing the stay is not workable, involve Airbnb support to discuss the available options rather than promising a particular relocation or refund outcome.

## Protect the calendar while the fault is unresolved

Look beyond the current reservation. Check the next check-in, any same-day turnover and every platform where the property is offered. Close unavailable inventory in the relevant selling calendars while you establish a reliable reopening point.

Airbnb's [calendar-sync documentation](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/99) explains that changes propagate according to the importing website's settings. After a maintenance closure, verify each destination calendar instead of assuming an immediate update.

## Keep refund decisions tied to the disruption

Airbnb's [rebooking and refund policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2868) considers severity, guest impact, the affected portion of the stay and evidence. It does not publish a fixed hot-water refund percentage.

Write down what was unavailable, for how long, what alternative was offered and what the guest agreed to. Avoid presenting a personal compensation formula as an Airbnb rule. For a host refund after check-in, Airbnb directs hosts to the [Resolution Center](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/165).

Never make a refund conditional on a positive review. Keep the repair discussion and any agreed remedy understandable on their own.

## Reopen after a verified handover

Before releasing the maintenance block, ask the responsible person to confirm that the reported fault has been resolved at the affected fixture and that the property is ready for use. Record who checked it and when; then tell the guest and update the next arrival's plan.

The cleaner's handover should distinguish “cleaning finished” from “maintenance cleared.” If access instructions changed during the visit, update the people who need them; the [late-night access playbook](/blog/self-check-in-failure-playbook) covers a separate arrival failure worth preparing for.
