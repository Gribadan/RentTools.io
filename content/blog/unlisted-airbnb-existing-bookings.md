You take your Airbnb listing off sale to get a break from new bookings. Then someone asks whether the cleaner should still come next week. Before cancelling that visit, check what you have actually paused: the route for new business or the work needed for guests already booked.

Airbnb’s [unlisting guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/476) says confirmed stays still need hosting after unlisting. The listing’s visibility and the status of an existing reservation are different things.

## Write down the boundary of the pause

Decide what you want the pause to achieve. You might want no additional bookings while completing the stays already accepted, or you might need a separate plan because the home cannot be used. These situations require different actions.

Record the property, the channel you changed and when you will review the decision. Do not describe the whole home as closed to the team if you only changed one listing’s visibility. A cleaner hearing “we have stopped hosting” may reasonably assume their future appointments are no longer needed.

If you cannot provide an accepted stay, address that reservation through the booking platform and the appropriate support process. Do not treat a hidden listing, a deleted local calendar entry or a note to the cleaner as resolution of the guest’s booking.

## Check the remaining reservations before removing work

Use the original booking records to identify the stays that remain confirmed. Check the correct property and the current arrival and departure arrangements. Then compare them with your coordination calendar.

For each remaining stay, answer three practical questions:

1. Who will prepare the home before arrival, and when is the visit agreed?
2. Who will provide the promised access and handle a time-sensitive guest question?
3. What work is needed after departure, and who will confirm it?

**Illustrative example:** a host pauses new sales with two confirmed stays still ahead. That does not establish a fixed number of cleaning visits. The plan depends on the home’s current condition, each handover and any already completed work. Check those tasks rather than deleting everything after the date the listing was paused.

If a reservation actually changes, use the [date-change cleaning check](/blog/reservation-date-change-cleaning-checklist). If it is cancelled, make a separate [decision about the cleaning visit](/blog/cancelled-booking-cleaning-decision) based on the verified cancellation and the work still needed.

## Keep the cleaner’s instruction specific

Tell the cleaner which appointments continue, which are being reviewed and who will confirm a change. Ask for acknowledgement through your agreed communication channel. A broad announcement about a pause is not a substitute for an agreed visit.

RentTools cleaning records support a date, status and notes. Keep the relevant record tied to the correct property and date, and use the note for the current instruction and next check. Saving it does not cancel an Airbnb booking, change a listing’s visibility or prove that the cleaner read it.

Leave unfinished preparation visible until its outcome is confirmed. Do not mark a visit complete simply to tidy the schedule for the break. If the reason for pausing is a fault that affects hosting, use the [maintenance-hold and reopening check](/blog/maintenance-calendar-hold-reopening) for the separate readiness decision.

## Verify every selling channel and plan the next review

If the same home is offered elsewhere, check that channel’s actual state and its existing bookings. Treat Airbnb visibility, calendar availability and the operational work list as separate checks.

Airbnb’s [calendar-sync guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/99) describes exporting and importing calendar information. Do not assume that a listing-visibility change has also changed another website’s listing status. Check that site directly.

Keep confirmed occupied nights protected while reviewing availability. If a calendar view looks different after the pause, reconcile it with the reservation source before allowing the nights to be sold again. Avoid disconnecting feeds merely to make an uncertain entry disappear.

Finally, choose who will review the pause and what must be checked before taking new bookings again. [Open RentTools](/onboard) and start with the next confirmed arrival: one clear cleaning instruction and one named person for the handover keep the break from becoming a missed stay.
