Two bookings sit next to each other on the calendar, apparently for the same guest. The first ends on the day the second begins. The cleaner expects a departure; the guest expects to leave their belongings in place. Neither expectation should be inferred from two adjacent bars.

Confirm what will actually happen at the boundary before changing the work plan. The reservation records describe booked nights. The cleaning decision describes the work you have agreed to do. Keep those two questions separate.

## Verify the bookings and the guest’s plan

Open both bookings at their sources. Check that they concern the same property, are confirmed and have the expected dates. Ask the booking guest whether the same party will remain in the home continuously, whether anyone changes and whether they expect to leave and return. A similar name or a generic imported title does not answer those questions.

**Illustrative example:** booking A runs from 4 to 7 December, three nights. Booking B runs from 7 to 9 December, two nights. Together they cover five nights, 4–8 December, with final departure on 9 December. The date 7 December is a record boundary; it is not evidence that the home will be empty for cleaning.

If the records might be duplicate representations of one stay, use the [duplicate-calendar-entry check](/blog/duplicate-calendar-entry-check) first. Do not treat a genuine second booking as a duplicate merely because the guest is familiar.

If the guest actually wants to change an existing reservation, use the booking channel’s formal process. Airbnb provides [date-change requests for confirmed home reservations](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/913). Read the resulting status rather than manually rewriting records to imitate an unconfirmed change. This workflow assumes you have verified two real bookings.

## Choose the work at the boundary explicitly

Ask what service, if any, has been agreed between the bookings. Check your commitments to the guest and the team before changing a scheduled visit. The same guest can still need cleaning; two reservations do not establish what service was promised.

Choose the instruction that fits the verified arrangement:

- **Actual departure and new arrival:** keep a turnover plan with a clear handover time.
- **Continuous occupancy with an agreed visit:** define the work, time window, access and permission.
- **Continuous occupancy with no visit agreed or required:** tell the team that the boundary visit will not take place, and confirm the final departure clean separately.

For an Airbnb stay, its [privacy policy for physical spaces](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/3060) requires guest permission for access during a reservation, except in an emergency. Treat a routine cleaning visit during continuous occupancy as something to arrange, not automatic permission to enter.

For the agreed service visit, use the [mid-stay cleaning workflow](/blog/mid-stay-cleaning-long-booking). If occupancy or service remains uncertain, leave the decision open with a named coordinator instead of silently removing the work.

## Record the cleaning outcome, keep the bookings

RentTools cleaning records have a date, status and notes, including pending, done and skipped statuses. On the relevant property and date, record the actual decision. If a visit is deliberately skipped, explain why and who confirmed the plan. Do not mark it done when no cleaning happened.

A cleaning record is for the property and date, so check any existing note before updating it. Preserve instructions that still apply to another task that day. A note does not merge bookings, obtain permission or notify the cleaner; send the instruction through the team’s normal channel and ask for acknowledgement.

Keep the two valid reservation records intact. Deleting one to remove an apparent cleaning task can make booked nights disappear from your working view. Resolve a wrong source record at its source; do not use housekeeping status to conceal a reservation problem.

## Recheck the final departure and next arrival

In the example, the final departure remains 9 December. Verify the next booking after it and the available preparation time. Do not cancel that departure clean simply because you skipped or replaced the visit on 7 December.

Use the current [cleaning schedule](/blog/cleaning-schedule-automation) for the handover and replace any old copy the team is following. Read back the boundary decision, final departure and acknowledged cleaning slot. If either booking changes, review the plan again.

Before the next pair of consecutive bookings, [open RentTools](/onboard), check both sources and leave one clear cleaning instruction for the shared date. A continuous stay can have two reservation records and one coherent work plan.
