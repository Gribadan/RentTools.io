A guest asks to stay one more night. The extra night appears free, so you agree in a message. But the cleaner still has tomorrow's departure on their schedule, and another platform has not yet picked up the new block. The reservation, the availability and the work plan now tell different stories.

Treat a date change as a small handover with three checks: what is confirmed, what can still be sold, and who will prepare the property. This workflow covers extensions, shorter stays and moved arrivals. The worked example is illustrative, not a report from a real property.

## Start with the confirmed reservation

Open the booking on the platform where it was made. Record the current arrival and departure, the proposed dates and the request status. A friendly agreement in chat is a reason to complete the formal process, not a substitute for checking its result.

For guest-initiated changes on Airbnb, use its [trip change request process](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1504). Check the resulting reservation details before updating your final handover.

Do not assume every change needs the same approval step. Airbnb describes [instant extensions for some Instant Book stays](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/50); unanswered host proposals leave the reservation unchanged. A payment failure can also reverse a change. Read the current status.

Keep a proposed cleaning slot provisional while a change is pending. If it is confirmed later, run the checks again rather than assuming yesterday's availability and staffing still hold.

## Check the two edges of the stay

An extension moves departure. A shifted arrival changes what must happen before check-in. A shorter stay can bring cleaning forward. Compare both the old and new dates with the reservations immediately before and after the stay, including direct bookings and owner blocks.

Use actual property times and the cleaner's available slot. A vacant night does not mean a cleaner is available the following morning. Likewise, a late arrival does not automatically make a delayed turnover acceptable: use the check-in time you have committed to.

**Illustrative example:** a stay originally runs from 8 to 11 November, three nights. It is extended to 12 November, four nights. The next booking begins on 12 November at 15:00 and checkout is 11:00. That leaves four hours. If your measured cleaning and inspection allowance is three and a half hours, the remaining margin is 30 minutes. These are assumptions for this example, not recommended cleaning times.

The original cleaner was booked for 11 November. Until someone accepts the 12 November slot, the extra night is operationally unresolved even though it fits on the calendar. If the new turnaround cannot be staffed, resolve that before agreeing to a change you control.

## Verify availability beyond the source calendar

After confirmation, inspect the changed nights in your combined calendar and each platform where the property can be booked. Check both the newly occupied nights and the nights released by a shorter or moved stay. Keep any cleaning or owner block that still has a purpose.

Airbnb's [calendar-sync guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/99) says exported changes depend on the receiving website's settings. Imported calendars refresh periodically; a connected feed is not proof that every destination already reflects this alteration.

If a mismatch remains, record the affected nights and latest refresh time. Use the platform's availability controls to protect nights you cannot honour, then verify the result. Track temporary manual blocks so they can be reviewed after the calendars agree. Do not remove a block merely because it looks like a leftover.

For diagnosing an import that stays out of date, use the [calendar-sync troubleshooting guide](/blog/airbnb-calendar-not-syncing). Here the completion test is specific: the changed booking and the sellable nights agree across the places you actually sell.

## Send a replacement cleaning handover

Give the cleaner one replacement instruction that identifies what it supersedes. Include the property, revised departure, cleaning window, next check-in deadline and any changed preparation needs. Ask for acknowledgement; sending the message does not establish that the new slot is covered.

An adaptable template:

> Schedule update: please replace the 11 November departure clean with 12 November, starting after 11:00 and ready before 15:00. The next arrival is unchanged. Please confirm that you can cover this slot; otherwise I will arrange cover. This replaces the earlier instruction.

Adapt the dates and times to the confirmed booking. Also review any departure reminders or access instructions you maintain separately, so they do not continue referring to the old stay.

## Close the change with a short read-back

Before marking the task done, read back the confirmed dates, the next arrival and the acknowledged cleaning slot. Note who checked the selling calendars and when. If one destination is still stale, leave a follow-up open with a named owner rather than calling the whole change complete.
