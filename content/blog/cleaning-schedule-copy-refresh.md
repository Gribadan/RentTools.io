The cleaner has a schedule you sent earlier in the week. Since then, a guest has changed dates, but the original message is still easy to find in the conversation. Updating the calendar does not replace that message or the paper on someone’s fridge.

The aim is not to send a fresh list every few minutes. It is to make sure the person doing the work knows when the old instruction has changed and which visit is now agreed.

## Make the copy’s scope clear

When sending a schedule, identify the property or group of properties and the period covered. Add a short label in your message saying when you checked it. That label is your handover note, not proof that all source bookings will remain unchanged.

A copied list, screenshot or printout keeps the content captured at that moment. It can remain useful offline, but it cannot show a change that happened afterwards. Decide where the current plan will be checked before a visit and how urgent changes will be communicated.

RentTools supports copying and printing the visible cleaning schedule. Review the actual output before forwarding it: a selected view may not be the full portfolio. The compact schedule does not carry the detailed record notes, so send any necessary task clarification separately through your agreed channel.

## Verify the change before moving the cleaner

Open the original reservation and establish what has actually changed. A guest asking for a different date is not the same as an agreed change. Compare the confirmed arrival and departure with the relevant property’s current calendar and cleaning plan.

Airbnb’s [calendar-sync guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/99) describes periodic updates of imported calendars. A recently copied schedule does not establish that every upstream change has already reached the view. Check the reservation source when the result matters for an imminent visit.

For a confirmed date change, use the [cleaning-plan date-change check](/blog/reservation-date-change-cleaning-checklist). For a cancellation, make the separate [decision about whether cleaning is still needed](/blog/cancelled-booking-cleaning-decision). Avoid deleting a visit merely because one calendar entry disappeared.

If the change makes it unclear whether the guest has left, follow the [departure-confirmation handover](/blog/checkout-confirmation-cleaning-handover). An old printed checkout date is not current permission to enter an occupied home.

## Replace the instruction, not just the attachment

State the change in the message itself before attaching or pasting the revised list. Name the affected property and visit, say what replaces the old instruction and make any remaining uncertainty explicit. Do not require the cleaner to compare two long schedules to discover one important change.

**Illustrative example:** an agreed checkout moves from Friday to Saturday. The host verifies that change in the reservation, checks the new cleaning arrangement and tells the cleaner that the Friday instruction is superseded. The Saturday visit is still a proposal until the person doing it confirms the arrangement.

Before treating the replacement as agreed, check three things:

1. Does the recipient know which earlier visit or instruction is replaced?
2. Have they confirmed the new date, time and practical access arrangement?
3. Is there a named person to resolve any remaining clash or unanswered question?

If the cleaner uses paper, ask them to replace or clearly mark the earlier sheet. If they use a message thread, identify the current version rather than leaving two contradictory lists without explanation. You do not need to erase the history to make the next action clear.

## Keep agreement and completion separate

RentTools cleaning records have a date, status and notes. Record the current instruction and an unresolved follow-up beside the correct property and cleaning date. Keep the human confirmation in your agreed communication channel; saving a note or generating another copy does not prove that anyone read or accepted it.

If no reply arrives, follow up or arrange a confirmed alternative before relying on the new visit. Do not mark work complete to indicate that a revised message was sent. Agreement about the appointment and confirmation that the home was prepared answer different questions.
