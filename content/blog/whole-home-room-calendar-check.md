You offer two bedrooms separately and also list the entire home. A whole-home booking arrives, but a room calendar still looks open. The problem is bigger than matching two listing names: the same bed may be included in more than one offer.

Before treating those calendars as independent properties, write down what each guest would actually receive. That gives you a way to check the booking protection and the cleaning plan without guessing what a coloured calendar block means.

## Map the space that cannot be sold twice

Start with the physical layout and the promises in each listing. Which bedrooms belong to the whole-home offer? Which entrances and facilities are shared? Can the room listings genuinely be hosted at the same time under their advertised arrangements?

**Illustrative example:** the whole-home offer includes Room A and Room B. Selling the whole home and either room for the same occupied night would promise overlapping space. The following is a required availability check for this example, not a report of what any calendar system has already done:

| Confirmed stay | Also check as unavailable |
|---|---|
| Whole home | Room A and Room B |
| Room A | Whole-home offer |
| Room B | Whole-home offer |

A Room A booking does not, by itself, tell you whether Room B can still be sold. That depends on the actual accommodation and what both listings promise. Do not turn a useful whole-home connection into an assumption that every room must block every other room.

Use distinct names that make the scope clear to your team. If a feed may belong to a different unit, complete the [property-mapping check](/blog/wrong-property-calendar-link-check) before changing availability.

## Check the Airbnb connection and its result

Airbnb’s [linked-listings guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1864) supports connecting listings within one home so a whole-home booking blocks linked rooms. Review the selected main listing and included listings in the live settings.

Then inspect the affected dates using a real confirmed reservation already on your calendar. Check the whole-home calendar and each included room separately. For a room reservation, verify the whole-home result as well; do not infer that direction from a successful whole-home check.

Write down which booking you checked, the affected nights and any destination that does not match the intended arrangement. Keep unresolved overlapping space unavailable while you investigate. Do not create a fake guest reservation merely to test a connection.

A saved setting is only part of the review. The result that matters is whether the same physical space can still be bought through a conflicting offer for those nights.

## Review external calendars without confusing blocks with guests

If a room or the whole home is also sold on another platform, include that destination in the check. Airbnb’s [calendar-sync guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/99) describes importing and exporting calendar information. Review each connected destination directly instead of assuming the internal listing relationship has configured the other website.

A protected date on a related listing is not automatically another guest arrival. Trace the original reservation before counting guests or arranging work. For an entry whose meaning is unclear, use the [blocked-date check](/blog/blocked-calendar-dates-not-reservations).

RentTools brings connected calendar information into a property-based view. Use it to compare the relevant dates and sources, but do not assume that similarly named properties automatically share an inventory rule. Keep each feed attached to the intended property and verify the platform calendars where the space is actually offered for sale.

If the same stay appears in several feeds, use the [duplicate-entry check](/blog/duplicate-calendar-entry-check) to distinguish repeated availability information from separate reservations. Do not delete a protective block simply to make the view look tidier.

## Give the cleaner the actual scope of the stay

Tell the cleaner which rooms were occupied, which guest is leaving and whether another room remains occupied. A whole-home departure and a Room A departure can require different access arrangements and preparation, even when their calendar entries cover similar dates.

RentTools cleaning records have a date, status and notes. Record the confirmed scope beside the correct property and cleaning date, with an owner for anything still uncertain. Contact the person doing the work through your agreed channel; a saved note does not prove that the instruction was read.

Keep the physical handover separate from the availability review. A blocked room is not evidence that it needs a second turnover clean, and a completed cleaning record does not prove that every selling calendar is protected.
