The reservation says two guests, but a message now says four. Adding two towels might seem to finish the job. Yet the second bed may still be unmade, the preparation brief may still say one bedroom, and the booking itself may still be waiting for approval.

A useful response connects three things: the confirmed reservation, the sleeping arrangement and the person preparing the home. This guide handles that handover before arrival. It does not set extra-guest charges or decide whether a property may accommodate a larger group.

## Start with the confirmed booking

Ask whether the new number is a request or an accepted change. Check the reservation on the platform where the stay was booked, including its guest categories. Do not infer the overnight party from the number of people invited into a message thread.

On Airbnb, a guest's request to add people needs the host's approval before it is confirmed. Its [guest-count change guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2369) also says a count change applies to the entire reservation. If someone joins for only part of a stay, clarify the actual overnight pattern in the booking conversation instead of assuming one number describes every night.

Check that you can accommodate the proposed group before accepting it. Airbnb describes a host's [trip-change request as the way to correct the count](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1507) on a confirmed reservation. Complete that platform step separately from your internal preparation notes.

## Turn a headcount into a room setup

Ask which sleeping spaces the group needs. Four people could require two double beds, a double and two singles, or another arrangement your listing actually offers. A headcount alone does not tell the cleaner which beds to make.

Write the preparation brief in concrete terms:

- **Beds:** which existing sleeping places to prepare and which linen belongs on each.
- **Bathroom supplies:** the agreed towel allocation, with any property-specific extras named explicitly.
- **Arrival information:** where the additional bedding is, or how an offered convertible bed is set up, if relevant.
- **Timing:** when the revised setup must be finished and who will check it.

For a smaller group, confirm preferences before removing prepared bedding or closing a room. Fewer guests can still need separate beds. For stock planning beyond this one stay, use the [linen inventory guide](/blog/linen-inventory-short-term-rental).

## Worked example: two guests become four

**Illustrative assumptions:** the home offers two double beds, the revised booking is accepted for four people, and they want both beds. Your own preparation standard provides one bath towel and one hand towel per guest. The original brief prepared one bed and two towel pairs.

The revised instruction is to make the second double bed and add two bath towels plus two hand towels. The finished setup has two made beds, four bath towels and four hand towels. It needs one additional bed's linen, not two extra complete bed sets simply because two people were added.

Send that instruction with the stay dates and readiness deadline. Ask the preparer to confirm that the additional linen is available and the changed task fits the work window. If it does not, agree a workable arrangement with the guest before promising it.

## Update the working record separately

In the RentTools reservation, review and save the booked guest count once the change is confirmed. This is an internal record update; do not treat it as acceptance of a platform alteration or a message to the guest or cleaner.

RentTools can use a booked count for its structured traveler section in guest forms. If you use that workflow, review the information already submitted against the revised party. A previously completed form is not evidence that the new travelers have been accounted for. Collect only what your actual process requires; this article does not prescribe identity-document or registration requirements.

Keep the preparation brief and any guest conversation consistent with the saved count. When dates also change, follow the [reservation date-change checklist](/blog/reservation-date-change-cleaning-checklist) rather than leaving the original cleaning window in place.

## Close the handover before arrival

A sent message is only the start. Get acknowledgement of the replacement brief, then confirm completion at the agreed time. For someone already working from a printed sheet or copied schedule, explicitly say that the new setup replaces the old one; updating an internal field does not replace their copy.

Use the [cleaning schedule guide](/blog/cleaning-schedule-automation) to review the broader turnover plan. For the next changed booking, finish with one short record: confirmed party, beds required, supplies to add, person responsible and readiness check. That is enough to turn a changed number into a prepared home.
