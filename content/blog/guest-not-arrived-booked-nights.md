Consider this illustrative situation: a guest expected in the evening has not sent an arrival message. The home is ready, tomorrow’s work is planned, and someone asks whether the unused nights can be sold again. You do not yet know whether the guest is delayed, already inside after self check-in, or no longer travelling.

Treat the missing message as an unresolved arrival, not a calendar instruction. Keep one person responsible for following it up, and separate contact attempts from changes to the reservation. This guide covers that operational handover rather than calculating no-show charges.

## Establish what is known and make contact

Open the reservation where it was booked. Check its current status, property, booked dates and the latest agreed arrival arrangements. Use the property’s local time when comparing messages; “after midnight” needs a date as well as a clock time.

Send a short message in the booking conversation asking whether the guest has arrived or needs help with access. If you use another permitted contact method, record the relevant outcome in the same conversation. A message marked sent is not confirmation that the guest read it, and silence does not establish whether the home is occupied.

Airbnb’s [guidance when a guest does not show up](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/309) suggests contacting the guest and contacting Airbnb for next steps if they remain unresponsive. Use the process for the actual booking channel; do not apply one platform’s reporting deadline to another.

**Sample message to adapt:**

> We have not yet confirmed your arrival. Have you checked in, or is your arrival delayed? Please reply here with your expected arrival date and local time, and let us know if you need help accessing the home.

This is illustrative wording, not a real guest exchange. If the reply reveals an access problem, switch to the [self check-in troubleshooting plan](/blog/self-check-in-failure-playbook). Do not continue treating an access failure as an unanswered travel update.

## Keep an arrival delay separate from booked nights

A later arrival time and a shorter reservation are different changes. Read the confirmed reservation before editing dates, releasing availability or promising the nights to someone else. A calendar block is not proof that a guest has physically arrived, just as no arrival message is not proof of cancellation.

**Illustrative dates:** the confirmed stay runs from 30 November to 3 December, three nights: 30 November, 1 December and 2 December. The guest later confirms arrival at 00:30 on 1 December. If the reservation itself is unchanged, its booked range remains 30 November–3 December. Do not shift the stored start date merely because the clock crossed midnight. These dates are an example, not an actual booking.

If the guest instead asks to shorten or move the stay, handle that through the booking channel’s change process and check its result. Airbnb describes [trip change requests for confirmed reservations](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1504). A discussion about different nights is a reason to check that process, not evidence that it is complete.

Once a change is confirmed, use the [date-change checklist](/blog/reservation-date-change-cleaning-checklist) to reconcile calendars and the turnover. Until then, keep the currently confirmed nights protected across selling channels. Do not delete a reservation or remove its block to make an uncertain arrival look tidy.

## Give the team one current plan and a follow-up owner

Tell whoever handles access what is confirmed and what remains unknown. If the guest gives a revised arrival time, check that the agreed access arrangement still works and ask the responsible person to acknowledge the plan. Do not make a cleaner’s entry decision from an unanswered message or an assumed empty room.

RentTools cleaning records have a date, status and notes. Use the relevant note for the operational instruction and the person coordinating the next check. It does not verify occupancy, contact the guest, classify a no-show or change a platform reservation. A note is also not proof that a teammate has read it; confirm the handover separately.

If the reservation is ultimately cancelled, review the [cleaning decision after cancellation](/blog/cancelled-booking-cleaning-decision) instead of automatically cancelling a visit. Preparation already done, an actual arrival and the next booking can lead to different work needs. Check the confirmed outcome at the source before reviewing any released nights elsewhere.

Close the follow-up only when you have a clear outcome: arrival confirmed with workable access, or a verified reservation change and a revised team plan. If contact is still unresolved, record who will check next and when. For your next delayed arrival, [open RentTools](/onboard), keep the confirmed dates intact and add one concise coordination note rather than turning silence into availability.
