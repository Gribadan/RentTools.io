A guest asks: “Can we leave two bags at 11:30?” Check-in is at 15:00 and the cleaner will still be working. A quick “yes” can leave three people with different plans: the guest expects access, the cleaner expects an empty room, and the host expects a brief handover elsewhere.

This is an illustrative situation, not a customer case. Decide what you can actually offer before answering. Storage, access to the home and readiness after cleaning are separate decisions.

## Check the place and the person before promising

Identify an actual place for the bags and someone who can receive them at the proposed time. Check that using that space is allowed under the arrangements that apply to your property. Do not promise storage on the assumption that a cleaner will accept responsibility when they arrive.

If the only available place is the room being cleaned, consider whether the visit will interrupt the job or conflict with another guest’s use of the home. Declining storage is clearer than making an arrangement the team cannot deliver. Do not treat a working entry code as an agreed luggage service.

Airbnb’s [guest ground rules](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2894) require prior host approval for arrival outside the check-in window. A luggage arrangement therefore needs an explicit description of what access you are approving.

## Write a small, complete handover plan

Confirm the bag count, drop-off point, time window, receiving person and collection arrangement in the booking conversation. Include whether the guest waits for someone, how to contact the coordinator and what happens if they arrive outside the window. Agree any charge before acceptance and check the applicable channel and storage terms; this guide assumes no price or coverage.

**Example wording to adapt:** “We can receive two bags at the agreed storage point at 11:30. Please message on arrival and wait for the handover. This is a bag drop only; check-in to the home remains at 15:00. We will confirm the collection arrangement here.” This is sample wording, not a real exchange. Replace the generic storage point with the actual agreed location.

Ask the guest to confirm the plan. Do not open bags or record their contents. Keep any handling record limited to what the handover needs, such as count, location and who received them.

## Brief the cleaner without changing readiness

Tell the cleaner whether guests will attend, where the bags will be and who coordinates them. Ask for acknowledgement; a note alone does not establish that the message was read. Keep bags away from the work route and supplies according to your agreed layout.

RentTools cleaning records contain a date, status and notes. Use the relevant note for the agreed visit and contact responsibility, alongside the team’s existing handover process. The [cleaning schedule](/blog/cleaning-schedule-automation) supplies context; it does not book luggage storage or message the guest automatically.

In the illustrative 11:30/15:00 situation, accepting bags at 11:30 does not complete the cleaning. Mark the work done only after it is finished, and check readiness separately. If the guest asks to stay inside, make a new decision rather than extending the luggage promise by implication. Use the [early-arrival workflow](/blog/guest-arrives-before-cleaning) if preparation is still underway.

## Close the handover or offer a verified alternative

Confirm who collected the bags and close the handling note. If a third person will collect, agree that handover with the guest in the booking conversation first. A changed arrival time should trigger a fresh check with the receiving person, not an assumption that someone will still be there.

If storage is unavailable, say so promptly and offer only options you have checked. Airbnb documents [Bounce storage through Trips where available](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/4210). The guest should verify the current location, hours and terms; availability is not universal. Do not send them to a closed or unconfirmed service.

For bags left after departure, agree the collection separately and keep the [checkout confirmation](/blog/checkout-confirmation-cleaning-handover) clear. Before the next request, [open your RentTools workspace](/onboard) and add a short handover note with one responsible coordinator.
