The cleaner finishes the rooms, but the spare cupboard has no toilet paper and the bathroom soap is almost empty. Someone placed an order yesterday; nobody knows whether it reached the property. The next arrival needs supplies in the right place, not a completed shopping task.

A restocking check closes that gap. Use it alongside cleaning, with one person responsible for confirming delivery and placement. This is a practical workflow, not an inventory feature or a consumption forecast.

## Define what this stay needs

Start with what you have promised in the listing and guest messages. Separate the items guests should find on arrival from the reserve the team uses to replenish them. A box in a locked cupboard is not the same as supplies available in the bathroom.

Airbnb's [essential-amenities guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2343) covers toilet paper, soap, towels, pillows and bed linen, with quantities suited to the group and stay. Use that guidance alongside your actual listing; do not assume one universal quantity fits every booking.

Write a short property-specific list: item, where it belongs, arrival target and reserve location. Check group size and stay length before preparing it. If the booking changes, revisit the [arrival-preparation checklist](/blog/guest-count-change-arrival-preparation). For a longer stay, agree how and when replenishment will happen rather than assuming the opening supply lasts indefinitely.

## Count usable stock, then calculate the gap

Count what is physically present and ready for use. Keep guest supplies, reserve supplies and incoming deliveries separate. Exclude damaged or unusable items. An order confirmation belongs in the delivery plan, not in the available-stock count.

**Illustrative example, not a platform requirement:** your own preparation list calls for six spare rolls at a specific location. Two usable rolls are there, so four must be added to reach six. Those numbers illustrate a count; they are not a recommended allowance per guest or night. If the four are taken from reserve, update that reserve count too.

Check at the beginning of the turnover, while there is still time to act. Have the person on site report shortages before leaving. Keep the list manageable: use the [cleaning schedule](/blog/cleaning-schedule-automation) to identify the relevant visit, and inspect the supplies rather than inferring them from cleaning status.

## Assign the missing items and confirm placement

For each gap, record the item, quantity, destination, responsible person and a deadline before arrival. Ask that person to acknowledge the task through your agreed team channel. If they cannot deliver in time, choose another confirmed plan immediately.

A useful note says what remains unresolved: “Example: add four spare rolls to the bathroom cupboard; coordinator to confirm placement before arrival.” Replace the generic role with your actual responsible person in your private workflow. This is sample wording, not a customer message.

RentTools cleaning records have a date, status and notes. A note can keep the shortage and follow-up beside the relevant visit. It does not count stock, place orders, send a delivery request or prove that anyone read it. Confirm those actions directly with the team. When cleaning is complete but restocking is outstanding, keep that distinction explicit in the note and your readiness check.

## Close the shortage before the guest discovers it

Confirm that the items arrived and were placed where expected. Close the outstanding note only after that check. If something promised will be unavailable, contact the guest promptly through the booking channel with the facts and the proposed next step.

Airbnb explains that [editing listing details after booking](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1506) does not update what confirmed guests see. Removing an amenity from the listing alone therefore does not communicate a shortage to an upcoming guest. Check the applicable platform process if the arrangement cannot be fulfilled.

After the turnover, review repeated shortages and adjust your own stock targets using actual counts. Use the [consumables-cost guide](/blog/consumables-cost-per-stay-math) for the separate budgeting question. For the next arrival, [open RentTools](/onboard), add one clear shortage note to the relevant cleaning record and name who will confirm it is resolved. Delivery is complete when the supplies are in place.
