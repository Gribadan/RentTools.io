The cleaner has finished the bathroom and kitchen, but the laundry delivery has not arrived. Check-in is approaching and the courier says the bags are on their way. That update helps with planning; it does not tell you whether the right sheets will reach the right home or who will put them on the beds.

Treat the delay as an unfinished preparation job with a specific owner. The immediate question is whether you can deliver the sleeping arrangement already agreed with the guest. Buying a larger linen reserve is a separate decision for after the turnover.

## Count the shortage at the beds

Ask the person at the property to check what is physically there. Identify the beds to be prepared, their sizes and the usable sheets, covers and pillowcases available for each. Include a sofa bed if it is part of the agreed arrangement. A sealed laundry bag elsewhere is not stock at this property.

**Illustrative example:** two beds each need one bottom sheet, one duvet cover and two pillowcases. That plan needs two sheets, two covers and four pillowcases. If only three usable pillowcases are present, the shortage is one pillowcase, even if a delivery note says “two sets”. These quantities describe this example, not a universal linen standard.

Check towels against the agreed guest count separately. Airbnb’s [essential-amenities guidance](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2343) recommends linens for each guest bed, plus a towel and pillow per guest. Check the amenities and quantities promised for the actual booking. Do not solve a shortage by quietly preparing fewer sleeping places. If the group has changed, use the [guest-count preparation check](/blog/guest-count-change-arrival-preparation).

## Arrange receipt and bed-making, not just dispatch

Ask the supplier to confirm the missing items and sizes, the destination and a current delivery estimate. Then confirm who can receive the bags. If the cleaner will have left, assign someone else to receive and finish the beds; do not assume the courier will do it.

Compare the expected arrival with the work still needed: receiving, checking the contents, carrying them to the rooms, making the beds and inspecting the result. Use the actual team’s estimate. A delivery five minutes before check-in is not automatically a workable preparation plan.

If that plan does not fit, check a real alternative: a complete usable reserve at the property or a confirmed collection from another source. Verify availability, fit, transport and the person doing the work before relying on it. Keep any borrowed items recorded so another property is not left short for its own arrival.

## Keep the unfinished work visible to the team

RentTools cleaning records have a date, status and notes. Put the shortage and handover beside the relevant visit. A note helps coordinate the work; it does not order laundry, track a courier or confirm that someone has read the plan.

**Sample internal note to adapt:**

> Example: one pillowcase still missing for the second bed. Coordinator to confirm receipt and bed-making before guest access. Keep the linen check open until both beds are inspected.

This is illustrative wording, not a customer message. In your private workflow, name the responsible person and the next check time. If the cleaner’s assigned work includes making the beds, that work is still unfinished. If a separate person owns the remaining step, make that handover explicit rather than treating a cleaning status as whole-home readiness.

## Confirm the result before promising access

Have someone check the actual rooms: all agreed beds made, correct pieces fitted, promised towels in place and delivery packaging cleared away. “Delivered”, “received” and “ready for the guest” are different outcomes. Close the outstanding note only after the last check.

If the promised arrangement will be unavailable or readiness is uncertain, tell the guest promptly through the booking conversation with the facts and a realistic next update. Do not silently move check-in or assume a listing edit informs this guest. Airbnb says [pending and confirmed guests do not see later listing changes](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1506). Use the applicable booking-channel process to agree any necessary change. If the guest is already there, follow the [arrival-before-cleaning plan](/blog/guest-arrives-before-cleaning).

After the turnover, review the cause: wrong contents, wrong size, missed receipt or insufficient usable reserve. Use the [linen-inventory guide](/blog/linen-inventory-short-term-rental) for the separate stock-planning question.
